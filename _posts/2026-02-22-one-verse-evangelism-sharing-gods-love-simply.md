Have you ever felt the Holy Ghost stirring your heart to share the good news of Jesus, but then felt overwhelmed by where to start? Many of us believe that to effectively share our faith, we need to memorize complex illustrations or have a seminary-level understanding of every scripture. But the truth is, **the gospel is most powerful when shared with love, clarity, and most importantly, simplicity.** God has given us a beautiful message that can be understood by all.

Today, we want to explore a powerful yet simple method called **One-Verse Evangelism.** This is an interactive way to share God's love, designed to be conversational and visual. It's incredibly easy to learn because it focuses on just one profound verse. You can share this life-changing message in as little as 10 to 15 minutes, yet its impact can last for an eternity.

One-Verse Evangelism is built on asking thoughtful questions and sharing God's truth in a straightforward manner. It's designed to make the gospel accessible, allowing you to share it anywhere, even on a simple piece of paper or a napkin.

Let's take a closer look at how this beautiful approach works. Imagine God is leading you to share the gospel with a neighbor or a friend. You can start by writing out a single, powerful scripture: **Romans 6:23.**

> "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord." — **Romans 6:23 (NIV)**

This one passage contains everything you need. After writing it down, you can simply ask your friend if they would like to see a simple diagram based on this verse that explains God's relationship with humanity, with us.

## Wages

Begin by circling the word "Wages" in the verse. Then, you can ask, "How would you feel if your boss refused to pay you the wages that were due to you?" Most people would agree they would want justice, the wages they had worked for. Deep down, we all understand that it is only right that we receive what we deserve. Similarly, we earn "wages" from God for how we have lived our lives.

## Sin

Next, draw a circle around the word "Sin." Ask your friend what comes to mind when they hear this word. You might explain that **sin is often more about an attitude than just an action.** It can be actively fighting against God, or it can be as simple as excluding Him from our lives. You could ask, "Has God ever seemed far away to you?" If they say "Yes," you can gently explain that this feeling of distance is one of the things sin does, it makes God seem far away. At this point, you can draw two opposing cliffs with a wide gap in between them, symbolizing the separation sin creates.

## Death

Now, circle the word "Death." Ask what thoughts come to mind when they hear this. Explain that in the Bible, **death always means some kind of separation.** In its most basic sense, it's separation from life itself. Because God is the author of life, a spiritual death simply means separation from Him. This is the consequence of our "wages."

## BUT

As you circle this small but mighty word, "BUT," mention that it is incredibly important. This word signals that a sharp contrast in ideas is coming. What we have just discussed is the challenging news, the reality of our condition. What comes next is the glorious good news!

## Gift

Draw a circle around "Gift." Ask, "If wages are what a person earns, then what is a gift?" Remind your friend that while every gift is free for the person receiving it, someone still has to purchase it or pay the cost.

## of God

Circle "of God" next. Explain that the gift you are talking about is truly free for us, and it comes directly from God Himself. It is so special and unique that no one else can give it. You might ask, "How do you feel when someone gives you a truly special gift?"

## Eternal Life

Circle these two words, "Eternal Life," and then ask, "How would you define these words?" Contrast one side of the cliff you drew earlier, representing "death" (separation from God), with the other side, representing "eternal life." Ask, "What do you think is the opposite of separation from God?" **Eternal life is to be forever in relationship with Him.**

## Christ Jesus

Now, write these words, "Christ Jesus," so they create a bridge spanning the gap between the two cliffs you drew. Help your friend to consider that just as every gift has a unique giver, **only Jesus Christ can give the gift of eternal life.** He is the bridge that closes the gap.

## Trust

Finally, write the word "Trust" over the bridge you just drew. Explain that a true friend is someone you can trust, and tell the person that Jesus is offering to be a true friend to them. All they have to do is admit that they are responsible for their sin, whether it's fighting against God or simply excluding Him from their life. **Trusting Jesus means believing that He has the power to forgive us for rejecting God and that He will wash us clean from all that we have done wrong in life.**

At this pivotal point, you can ask your friend if they want to start a relationship with God that will last forever. If they say "Yes," invite them to pray a short prayer in their own words, asking Jesus to forgive them and make them new.

Close by reminding your friend that this simple illustration beautifully shows what God is truly like: He is someone who genuinely cares about people, and He especially cares about them. Invite them to read all about it in the Bible. The Gospel of John is a wonderful place to start their journey of discovery! **God's love is real, and He desires a relationship with you.**
