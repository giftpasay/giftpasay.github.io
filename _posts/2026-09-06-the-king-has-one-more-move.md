 But even in everything we are facing, we still have victory.

## Stand Still And See The Salvation Of The Lord

 Let us read from Exodus 14.

> "And Moses said unto the people, Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD, which he will shew to you today: for the Egyptians whom ye have seen today, ye shall see them again no more for ever.
> The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace."
> **Exodus 14:13-14**

 Are you aware of that, mga kapatid?

 The Lord is fighting for us.

 Ang Diyos ay nakikipaglaban para sa atin. Hindi tayo nag-iisa. Hindi tayo iniwan ng Panginoon sa gitna ng digmaan. The Bible says, "The Lord shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace."

 There are moments when God tells His people, "Fear not." Huwag kang matakot. Stand still. Tumayo ka lang sa pananampalataya. Tingnan mo ang kaligtasan ng Panginoon na Kanyang ipapakita sa iyo.

 The Egyptians whom ye have seen today, ye shall see them again no more forever.

 There are enemies, situations, pressures, and battles that God can end in such a way na hindi mo na sila muling makikita gaya ng dati.

 God is alive, and the Bible tells us that He will do something.

 Vengeance belongs to the Lord.

 Hindi tayo tinawag na maging bulag sa mga nangyayari sa bansa natin, sa buong mundo, at sa paligid natin. We can see how the enemy is trying to move. Nakikita natin kung paano niya sinusubukang guluhin ang mga anak ng Diyos.

 Tayo lang naman talaga ang gusto niyang atakihin. The people of God are the target of the enemy.

 But our King is doing something.

 Vengeance belongs to the Lord.

 Let me preach on this subject today: **The King Has One More Move**.

## The Chess Painting Called Checkmate

 There is a story about a painting. Ang title ng painting ay **Checkmate**.

 Sa painting na iyon, makikita mo ang isang chess game. Parang ang kalaban ng tao ay ang devil. The devil was smiling. Nakangiti siya, because it seemed like he had the man in checkmate.

 Sa chess, kapag sinabing checkmate, tapos na. Wala nang move. Wala nang paraan. The king is trapped. The game is over.

 So in that painting, the devil seemed to be the obvious victor. Parang siya na ang panalo. Parang wala nang magagawa ang tao. Parang tapos na ang laban.

 But there was a chess grandmaster who looked closely at the painting.

 Hindi lang niya tiningnan nang mabilis. Pinag-aralan niya ang arrangement ng pieces sa chess board. Tiningnan niya kung nasaan ang king, nasaan ang kalaban, ano ang possible moves, at ano ang sitwasyon ng game.

 And while he was studying the painting, the grandmaster realized something.

 The man who seemed to be losing was not really losing.

 He was in fact not yet defeated.

 Why?

 Because according to the arrangement of the chess board, **the king still had one more move**.

 May isa pang pagkilos na pwedeng gawin ang king. May isa pang move na pwedeng magbago ng buong laro. If that move was made in the right direction, the one who seemed defeated could still become the winner.

 The grandmaster called the creator of the painting and said that the title **Checkmate** did not fit the scene.

 Hindi bagay ang title.

 Why? Because it was not yet checkmate. The enemy looked confident, but he had not really won. The man looked defeated, but he was not yet finished.

 The king still had one more move.

 Ganun din sa buhay natin.

 Minsan parang obvious na panalo na ang kaaway. Minsan parang nakangiti na siya sa sitwasyon natin. Minsan parang sinasabi niya, "Tapos ka na. Wala ka nang magagawa. Checkmate ka na."

 Minsan dahil sa problema, discouragement, pressure, sickness, pain, accusation, o mga pangyayaring hindi natin maunawaan, parang wala na tayong susunod na hakbang. Parang ubos na ang lakas. Parang wala na ang pag-asa.

 But the message of the Lord today is this:

 **The King has one more move.**

 Our King is not finished.

 Hindi nauubusan ng pagkilos ang Panginoon. Hindi Siya nauubusan ng pamamaraan. Hindi Siya nauubusan ng pwedeng gawin.

 Kung sa paningin natin parang talo na tayo, sa paningin ng Diyos hindi pa tapos ang laban.

 Kung sa paningin ng kaaway parang hawak na niya ang sitwasyon, hindi niya hawak ang huling salita.

 The King still has one more move.

 Ang Diyos natin ay buhay.

 Naniniwala ba kayo na ang Diyos natin ay buhay? Naniniwala ba kayo na ang Diyos natin ay aktibong kumikilos sa ating kapanahunan ngayon?

 I believe God is alive in this church. I believe God is not sleeping.

 Even when the devil tries to twist our minds, even when the enemy tries to disturb our faith, still our God is moving.

 Jesus said:

> "For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them."
> **Matthew 18:20**

## Pharaoh Marched After Them

 In Exodus 14, Israel had already come out of Egypt.

 They had already experienced deliverance. Nakalabas na sila mula sa pagkaalipin. They had seen the hand of God. They had seen miracles. They had seen how the Lord delivered them.

 But Pharaoh and his army marched after them.

 Merong humahabol sa kanila. Merong sumusunod sa kanila. The enemy wanted to bring them back to the place they had already left.

 Ganun din tayo minsan.

 The Lord already saved us. The Lord already delivered us. The Lord already brought us out. Dati tayong makasalanan. Dati wala tayong direksyon sa buhay. Dati sumusunod lang tayo sa agos ng mundo, hindi natin alam na papunta tayo sa kapahamakan.

 But thank God for the Word. Thank God for the ministry. Thank God for the Spirit of the Lord that moved in our lives. Thank God for the preaching of the Word of God.

 Nagkaroon tayo ng direksyon. Nagkaroon tayo ng buhay. Nagkaroon tayo ng kaligtasan.

 But still, the enemy does not stop.

 Hangga't wala pa tayo sa promised land, hangga't nagpapatuloy pa tayo, gumagawa pa rin ang kaaway upang tayo ay ma-discourage at maibalik sa dati.

## The Widow And The Unjust Judge

 Jesus told a parable about a widow in a city who came to an unjust judge.

 She said:

> "Avenge me of mine adversary."
> **Luke 18:3**

 Ipaghiganti mo ako sa aking kalaban.

 She came to the judge again and again. The judge did not fear God. He did not regard man. Wala siyang takot sa Diyos, and he did not care about people.

 For a while, ayaw niya siyang tulungan.

 But the widow kept coming.

 Paulit-ulit siyang lumalapit. Paulit-ulit siyang humihingi ng tulong. Paulit-ulit niyang dinadala ang kanyang request.

 And finally, the unjust judge said:

> "Though I fear not God, nor regard man;
> Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me."
> **Luke 18:4-5**

 Sabi niya, dahil kinukulit ako ng babaeng ito, ipaghihiganti ko na lang siya.

 It pays to be persistent.

 May halaga ang patuloy na pagdulog sa Panginoon. If an unjust judge can respond to persistence, how much more our just and righteous God?

 The Lord said, "Hear what the unjust judge saith."

 If even an unjust judge eventually answered, shall not God avenge His own elect who cry day and night unto Him?
